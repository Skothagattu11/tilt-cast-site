Under the hood

Live. Not
emulated.

Three things happen simultaneously the moment you connect.

01

Screen streams at 60 fps

USB or Wi-Fi carries the raw pixel buffer from your iPhone to TiltCast. Near-zero latency; the 3D model's texture updates every frame.

02

Gyroscope data drives rotation

CoreMotion on the device sends attitude data over the same connection.USB or wireless. The 3D model's yaw, pitch, and roll match the physical phone exactly.

03

You hit record and move naturally

Hold the phone how you'd hold it in a demo. Tilt it, pass it between hands, set it down. Every movement becomes cinematic.

Motion modes

How it
moves.

Three modes cover every use case. Switch mid-session without stopping the recording.

float mode

Float

subtle · ambient

Gentle breathing motion driven by sine waves at incommensurate frequencies. Feels alive without being distracting; ideal as a loop.

showcase mode rec

Showcase

dynamic · wide

Larger amplitude rotation that displays all angles of the device. Use it when you want the phone to command attention: hero sections, trailers.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Which iPhones work?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Connect via USB-C, Lightning, or wirelessly over Wi-Fi on the same network. iPhone 12 and newer recommended for best gyro accuracy.

Does it record my actual app or just an animation?

Your actual app. The screen is streamed live; whatever you do on the phone (tap, swipe, type) is captured in real time. It is not a static screenshot or a GIF playback.

Do I need a developer certificate to stream my screen?

No. TiltCast uses the standard iOS screen recording API, the same one Xcode Simulator uses. No provisioning profile or App Store binary required.

Is there a watermark on the free tier?

No watermark. Free exports include a subtle TiltCast callout in the corner that can be cropped. Pro removes it entirely.

Can I use TiltCast to record an Android phone?

Not yet. Android support is on the roadmap; sign up for the waitlist via the download page.

What frame rate and resolution does the stream run at?

Up to 60 fps at the phone's native resolution, USB or Wi-Fi. The 3D render target matches your Mac's display resolution, up to 4K on Pro.
